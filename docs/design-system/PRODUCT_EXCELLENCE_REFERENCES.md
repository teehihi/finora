# FINORA: expanded consumer-fintech reference review

Reviewed 26 November 2026. Companion to the sprint's existing MoMo, Vietcombank, Techcombank and Monzo review; not a product ranking. I rendered and inspected 54 publisher-supplied iPhone App Store tiles (six per product), two Cake help walkthroughs, TPBank's payment confirmation example, and three Zalopay help-flow screens. Store pages are regional and are promotional compositions, even when they embed recognizable application UI. Help screenshots are stronger evidence of a pictured flow, not proof of the current authenticated runtime, success/failure behavior, animation, or any offer in Vietnam. Do not reuse screenshot artwork, marketed balances, rates, awards or promises.

## Native/mobile acceptance checklist

- **Navigation and visual identity:** keep the approved FINORA dock and its usable platform fallback. Apple places Liquid Glass in the controls/navigation layer; in-content quotes and results should remain legible on solid surfaces. Check the final row clears the dock, as well as bottom-sheet/keyboard clearance. [Apple Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) · [Apple Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout)
- **One clear action:** contextual sheets should have a clear title, dismissal path and a primary action meaningful for the current state; avoid nesting sheets or combining explicit confirmation with a silent commit. Keep onboarding short; ask for inputs when the user can see why they are needed. [Apple Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets?changes=_1) · [Apple Onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding?changes=_7)
- **Accessible VND inputs:** persistent field labels, clear units and locally intelligible grouping, an appropriate keyboard, intact values on validation or retry, inline errors and a correction route. Missing fee is not zero; presenting both as “0 ₫” can make a comparison false. Check 44 × 44 pt iOS and 48 × 48 dp Android hit areas (including icon-only chips/dock tabs), large text, visible focus, contrast and semantic state labels. [Apple Text fields](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields) · [Apple Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility?changes=____5_8_2) · [Android accessibility guidance](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps)
- **Readable comparison, provenance and feedback:** align the monthly and total repayment, rate basis/method, assumptions and completeness at equal principal/term; keep full schedule secondary. Show lender/source on sourced offers and explicitly distinguish demo, user-entered, AI-extracted, missing and verified values. A chart needs numeric labels or an equivalent detail view; neither chart meaning nor delinquency may rely on color alone. Loading/failure should retain user-entered data and offer a meaningful retry; use motion as feedback and respect reduced-motion preference. [Apple Charts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/charts) · [Apple Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion)

## Verification boundary

The gallery evidence covers visual structure and content hierarchy, not interaction latency, accessibility conformance, lenders' eligibility, pricing or actual app behavior today. No authenticated competitor accounts were accessed. No product artwork is licensed or proposed for inclusion in FINORA. Research assets are inspection-only. Prioritize screenshot review of FINORA itself after implementation across mobile width, dark/large text, loaded/empty/error and keyboard-open states.

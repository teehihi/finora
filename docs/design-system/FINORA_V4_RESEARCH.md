# FINORA 4.0 — shipped fintech UI research

Reviewed November 26, 2026. This is a review of published application captures, official product/help pages, app-store listings and first-party tutorials, not hands-on use of third-party accounts. Images are references, not FINORA assets. Older captures are marked where notable; they supply interaction patterns, not claims about the current default interface.

## Principal benchmarks

### MoMo — familiar task hierarchy and a useful branded assistant

[Official MoMo Home and product screens](https://www.momo.vn/tai-app) · [Actual Home shown in official QR tutorial](https://www.momo.vn/thanh-toan-quoc-te) · [Official AI tour](https://www.momo.vn/tro-thu-tai-chinh) · [Profile redesign](https://www.momo.vn/tin-tuc/khuyen-mai/tab-toi-tren-momo-co-dien-mao-moi-9088)

The Home capture separates four everyday actions, account summaries, a deeper service grid, and later promotional/contextual content. The icon grid is readable because each symbol has a concrete short label; support and account controls are not competing with this first-view task hierarchy. The finance and spending screens frame numbers around specific jobs. MoMo’s profile update also maintains a single familiar entry point for settings, security, owned items and support.

**Apply:** Four quick actions with text (Tìm khoản vay, Mô phỏng, Đọc hợp đồng, Lịch trả nợ), one distinct Fin entry with a real next action, grouped settings. Avoid importing MoMo pink, loyalty/module sprawl, balance or QR button semantics, or a floating chat bubble that fights the approved dock.

### Techcombank Mobile — financial emphasis without forcing a desktop layout

[Official June 2026 redesign and before/after screens](https://techcombank.com/thong-tin/thong-bao/techcombank-ra-mat-giao-dien-hoan-toan-moi-nang-tam-trai-nghiem-tai-chinh-so) · [Official captured Home, older UI](https://techcombank.com/en/information/updates/the-first-online-indirect-investment-capital-account-iica-in-vietnam) · [Official savings/detail screenshots](https://techcombank.com/thong-tin/blog/rut-tien-tiet-kiem-truoc-thoi-han)

The legacy Home capture uses a high-emphasis money figure, one branded surface and a separate action rail; the 2026 release explicitly reorganizes quick access and anchors bottom navigation for one-hand reach. Updated account/cards captures show grouped product families, figures and operations without making product categories look like adverts.

**Apply:** A forest capacity hero with one number, an “ước tính” label and access to its assumptions. Lender pages prioritize visible amounts and conditions, then source and secondary actions. Do not copy tier-based styling, banking balance labels, decorative gradients across every screen or functions FINORA does not have.

### Revolut — reduce the distance from information to action

[Official launch notes](https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_launches_revolut_10_as_it_targets_primary_accounts_and_passes_35m_customers_worldwide/) (v10, 2023) · [Shipped v10 two-screen capture](https://www.helpmycash.com/blog/tu-banco-a-tu-estilo-con-la-nueva-app-revolut-10-diseno-personalizado-mas-herramientas-y-mayor-sencillez/) (dated independent capture) · [Current US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/revolut-send-spend-and-save/id932493382) · [Official loan navigation](https://help.revolut.com/help/credit-products/loans/applying-for-a-personal-loan/)

The dated two-screen capture uses one clearly selected financial context followed by compact actions and activity. The release documents one-tap product entry, meaningful context switching and dedicated planning spaces. The official loan flow supports discovery through Home search as well as navigation to credit products.

**Apply:** Tap from estimate to its basis, from an upcoming repayment to the tracked plan, and from one scenario to an authorized comparison. Give financial detail a predictable back path and retain navigation context. FINORA is not an account, wallet, disbursement service or credit approval tool.

**Reproducible visual samples:** [MoMo Home](https://homepage.momocdn.net/img/screen-2-261021111756.webp), [MoMo spending](https://homepage.momocdn.net/img/screen-4-261021111755.webp), [Techcombank dashboard annotations](https://techcombank.com/content/dam/techcombank/public-site/com_img/0805-giao-dien-moi-pic2.jpg.rendition/cq5dam.web.1280.1280.webp), [Cake loan entry](https://static.cake.vn/homepage/B1_Vay_nhanh_4faf9053ab/B1_Vay_nhanh_4faf9053ab.png), [Timo create-goal control](https://timo.vn/wp-content/uploads/How-to-set-up-a-Goal-Save_.png), [Copilot dashboard layer](https://www.copilot.money/img/home/hero/screen-clean.webp). App captures and illustrated first-party guides were visually checked; image copies are deliberately not bundled.

## Applying the research to the three Home explorations

Use the same 390px fixture, approved FINORA lockup and Fin, and unchanged dock across concepts. Score first-view financial comprehension, action recognition, mobile spacing, data honesty, and consistency with deeper routes. These are design hypotheses to judge after rendering, not rendered outcomes.

1. **Everyday control center** (leading hypothesis): forest monthly repayment-capacity hero, detached four-action row, one branded Fin insight banner, next real installment. Strong MoMo/Techcombank synthesis; avoid repeating metrics in the banner.
2. **Plan-first timeline:** clearly named tracked loan and next due amount/date first, followed by status-separated activity and a compact capacity summary. Useful for returning borrowers; needs a purposeful alternative for people with no tracked plan.
3. **Financial snapshot:** neutral surface, income/expense/current debt allocation with labels, then two contextual activities. A calmer Revolut/Monzo-inspired reading order, but action recognizability and first-view urgency need to survive.

These signals produce a distinct FINORA design: forest and white for branded financial focus, lime only for small accents, grouped plain-language labels, visible finance assumptions, no decorative account/bank fiction. Use generated data only in clearly labelled fixtures. In the 15M income, 8M expenses, 1M existing monthly debt fixture, 6M is the raw monthly remainder, **not** FINORA’s separate API-calculated repayment room. The first installment amount is a specific ledger entry, not the sum of all upcoming entries. Provide meaningful loading, unavailable, empty, and populated treatments.

## Typography and platform controls

- [Be Vietnam Pro](https://github.com/bettergui/BeVietnamPro) is purpose-built for Vietnamese; [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) is a neutral UI family; [Geist](https://vercel.com/font) suits compact financial UI and is already present in the existing export; [Apple system text](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) is a good native reference and fallback. Inspecting the actual font tables using “Ước tính khả năng trả nợ — 1.250.000 ₫”: the Google Fonts Inter variable file covers all characters and declares `tnum`/`pnum`; Be Vietnam Pro Regular covers all characters, has differing default digit widths, and does not declare those features; exported Geist 400/500 covers the phrase and declares both features, but the exact files lack U+20AB (₫). Geist can remain the shipped family if its symbol fallback and tabular values are verified in rendered results. Inter is a single-family alternative if all-Vietnamese glyph coverage with tabular numerals is preferred and the proper font is bundled. Verify accessibility-size wrapping as well as coverage.
- [Apple’s Liquid Glass adoption guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/TechnologyOverviews/adopting-liquid-glass) and [materials guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) support a separate control layer. Keep the existing drag/tap/navigation-on-release behavior, states/labels, screen-reader handling, and reduced-motion path. Browser and Android fallback are not native Liquid Glass. Verify iOS native rendering on a supported simulator/device if available rather than inferring it from web.
- [WCAG text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum) and [target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum): test contrast for white text over every hero-gradient stop and for neutral/small text on actions and dark surfaces; prefer 44pt native targets where practical. Use labels with colors for financial status or composition and meaningful focus indicators on web.

### Practical transfer checklist

- **Discovery & detail:** distinguish published terms from calculated scenarios. Summaries should show lender name, amount/term range, rate basis, meaningful fee availability and source. Use text with icon indicators. Expanding extras must never hide primary assumptions.
- **Simulator & comparison:** a labeled amount input with limits and a bounded interaction, then a clearly defined monthly estimate. Stack corresponding terms in the same order on mobile. On desktop use two side-by-side scenario columns, not a shrunken dense table on narrow widths.
- **Assistant & document flows:** give Fin one natural invitation grounded in the task; use structured numeric outputs with source and context, not an unbounded chat bubble. Use an explicit filename/review step and extraction status before showing findings, and preserve the correction/confirmation path. Attribute published and extracted data; keep missing data visible.
- **Authentication & settings:** one active step at a time, touch-friendly action order, error and progress messages near the relevant control, no placeholder provider buttons. Give account, appearance, privacy, language and sign-out distinct groups.
- **Activity:** distinguish due, upcoming, paid and simulated; confirm a user-marked payment and explain that it does not move money. On an empty account show real actions rather than fake monetary values.

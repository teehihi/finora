## Three 390 × 844 explorations

- **A — Capacity-first.** Branded financial estimate, five practical tools, near-term activity, then Fin. The cleanest introduction for people who have not borrowed yet; an active borrower can still see the next repayment without digging.
- **B — Repayment-first.** A dated installment timeline gets the first section; affordability moves to an unboxed secondary panel. Useful for an existing borrower, but too empty on a first visit and overly biased toward after-borrowing.
- **C — Fin-first.** A branded concierge header and mascot lead to chat, with estimates below. Clear assistant discovery, but asking for help becomes a gate in front of repeat calculations and tracked obligations.

Selected **A**. Explorations are synthetic design renders, not production screenshots. In the first app render, the two-line labels caused the three portfolio amounts to appear on different baselines. In the second render, values precede captions and the keyboard/touch actions have slightly larger labels; the allocation is omitted when expenses and debt exceed income rather than showing a misleading full-width equivalent. Originally Home looked only at the rolling 30-day ledger; the actual next unpaid installment now also appears if it is farther away.

## Verification

- Sources of truth: `GET /api/account` for entered values and the server's estimate; `GET /api/repayments` for tracked installments; `GET /api/simulations` for simulation previews. No bank balance, credit approval or guaranteed borrowing terms are shown.
- Demo catalog and simulator shortcuts use labeled sample routes; the loan-finding shortcut goes to the public catalog. The heading still distinguishes monthly payment capacity from remainder before new borrowing.
- Visual evidence was captured from the actual exported app using synthetic, intercepted API data at 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, and 1440px (see sprint handoff). Native docking, Dynamic Type and touch remain to be confirmed on devices.

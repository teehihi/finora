# Design exploration · Finora 2.0

These are six local design prototypes, not screenshots of running banking or auth flows. Each direction tests Home and the entry point to passwordless authentication; the 3.400.000 ₫ against which the layouts are compared is clearly sample data. See the [source](screenshots/exploration/prototypes.html), [desktop comparison (1440 px)](screenshots/exploration/directions-1440.webp) and [mobile comparison (390 px)](screenshots/exploration/directions-390.webp).

| Visual direction                           | Home / authentication                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Review and decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **I. Sổ tay tài chính** · Editorial ledger | Ivory canvas, short Source Serif 4 heading, stronger forest panel for the monthly estimate, then plain ledger rows. Authentication continues the same voice with a left-aligned labeled email form and a secondary password path. | Selected. The number and its estimate label read together at both sizes. At 390 px the headline wraps gracefully; email and CTA retain their own lines. Calm and recognizably Finora. The forest proportion needs restraint and literal amounts must appear only when the calculation exists.                                                           |
| **II. Nhịp tiền** · Consumer fintech       | Geist-first hierarchy, brighter numeric focus, comparison bar, more guided auth framing.                                                                                                                                          | Borrow the precise numbers and direct labeling, not the lime focal surface and prominent chart. The worked desktop comparison was quick to scan; at 390 px the bar did not justify its space without both values and the corresponding assumptions. An announced two-step flow becomes redundant when configured social login may complete in one step. |
| **III. Cùng Finora** · AI-native workspace | The financial question starts the hierarchy; the answer is visually tied to the numerical result. A compact, softer sign-in form leads with privacy.                                                                              | Borrow the threaded result structure for the dedicated assistant, not the default Home. At 390 px question, estimate and action compete for space. Requiring a prompt to discover useful financial facts makes the product harder to use when no saved history or real AI provider exists.                                                              |

The selected direction uses one decision-oriented focal object per screen. Wide product layouts can add a spatially quiet companion column without consuming the reading column. Authentication instead separates a brand-side introduction from the actual account flow. This applies the patterns from [external research](DESIGN_RESEARCH.md) without copying another product's marketing or assets.

The source is a static HTML comparison; inputs are intentionally non-functional. Open locally after installing workspace dependencies to use Finora's same typefaces and original lockup. Screenshot evidence here is separate from before/after screenshots of the real app.

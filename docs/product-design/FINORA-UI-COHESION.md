- Baseline: merged main `8d223fe`. Home and Fin chat are the approved references and remain unchanged, including their images, status bar, keyboard, dock and navigation.
- New work: Explore, Loans and Profile get distinct photography. Cashflow and Document Intake each get their own smaller illustration; editing and saved result pages keep focused, compact tool chrome. The public intro and authentication retain their separate existing identity.
- Profile uses a photographic cover and overlapping account monogram, plus four direct shortcuts for finances, sign-in, preferences and privacy. They jump to the existing sections; no account settings, authentication operations or writes changed. The photo is decorative, not an editable account asset.
- Market source, estimates and tracked obligations stay distinct. Scope excludes backend, API, legal/financial copy, formulas, packages, credentials, consent and any release.
- Design review used the existing composition as the style reference, followed by individually generated assets, 390/320 px phone screenshots and desktop browser checks. Sources were created with the built-in ImageGen tool, visually inspected, and resized/compressed without compositing text into the files. These are illustrative images, not claims about actual locations or unaltered documentary photos.

## Artwork and final prompts

The committed assets live in `apps/app/assets/scenes/workspace/`. The profile asset is inspired by riverside gardens in Cần Thơ; the scenery is not represented as a verified image of a particular property.

### `discover-market.jpg`

Landscape 3:2, editorial photorealistic architecture for a premium Vietnamese finance app Explore tab. Interior of iconic Bến Thành Market, Ho Chi Minh City, seen from outside at quiet early morning; recognizably warm ochre colonial clock-tower façade on the RIGHT HALF, mellow sunlight, pale ivory and jade green, subtle motorbike silhouette only if tiny, real details but no readable signage anywhere. Left 48% is bright plain warm atmospheric negative space and no objects, for overlaid dark UI copy later. Horizontally compose an attractive extra wide 3:1 crop around the center and keep the important clock tower in the middle vertical half. Real photo, sophisticated no illustration, not a skyline, no text, no logos, no borders, no screen mockup. This image alone is the asset.

### `loans-canal.jpg`

Landscape 3:2 background art only for a premium Vietnamese finance app repayment tab. A real-looking Bến Tre coconut canal with a small concrete bridge spanning from center to the right, seen at waterside; lush overlapping coconut fronds and their rhythmic reflections only on the far right, the feeling of a dependable route and calm water. Cinematic jade/teal greens with the warm golden edge of late afternoon, photography not painting, authentic southern Mekong delta rather than skyline. Left 45% pale blue-green reflective atmosphere kept quiet for dark UI text; main object in middle vertical half for a 3:1 landscape crop. No text, logos, decorative UI or frame.

### `profile-riverside.jpg`

Landscape 3:2 photographic COVER IMAGE ONLY for a modern Vietnamese fintech personal profile. Cinematic Southern Vietnamese riverside garden, a jacaranda-like lavender blossom branch and warm glancing sun on the water, architectural glimpse of the historic Bình Thủy house garden in Cần Thơ if natural. Jade, soft lilac and peach sunrise; intimate, personal, editorial, not a busy city or identical to a generic fintech banner. Distinct horizontal flowing composition with sunlight/flower detail at the upper-right and center; lower-left gently shaded and quieter where a circular avatar will overlap the edge later. Keep the memorable central details usable in a 3:1 crop. No people, avatar, lettering, logo, or UI, no frame.

### `documents-glass.jpg`

Landscape 3:2 designed photo artwork, no text, for Vietnamese fintech screen that privately reads a loan document. Refined cinematic top-down still life, semitransparent frosted green glass loupe over layered off-white paper with no legible marks, smooth Forest-green desk with sparse softly textured fine grain, natural southeast Asian window light. Objects kept on RIGHT HALF and center vertical band with negative space on the left for practical UI and ergonomic 3:1 cropping. Calm, trustworthy, not a stock photo of signing, no hands, no logo, no UI.

### `cashflow-fields.jpg`

Landscape 3:2 raster photographic accent only for a Vietnamese fintech cash-flow map. Aerial drone photograph of a winding waterway traversing immaculate emerald and sage rice fields in An Giang, Southern Vietnam; sections create visual rhythm like a gentle chart but image remains a true landscape, river turns on the right, horizonless bright early light, left quiet and muted. Keep most distinctive curve in the center vertical half for a panoramic crop. No labels or overlaid graphics, no logos or UI.

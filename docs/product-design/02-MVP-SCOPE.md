# 02 · MVP scope for the design sprint

**Objective:** Protect the competition demonstration's borrowing-decision story, keep the rest reachable, and avoid feature inflation. See [inventory](02-FEATURE-INVENTORY.md) and [acceptance stories](02-USER-STORIES.md).

## Must-have: one coherent decision

- Public example before sign-in, clear OTP/session recovery, optional onboarding and working settings/privacy.
- Home surfaces current situation and a clear next action without pretending incomplete inputs are facts.
- Vietnamese Finora AI assists with clarifying intent, filling minimum assumptions and interpreting deterministic outputs. A locally verified workflow must work without provider credentials.
- Explore, published offer detail, fair A/B comparison, manual simulation, costs/uncertainty and deliberately saved estimate.
- Distinct language for estimates and tracked obligations. Confirm before persistence and keep the current protected backend, math, owner checks and existing dock.

## Should-have: demonstrate the lifecycle when time allows

- Contract evidence review with synthetic fixture and readable manual fallback.
- Confirm an actual tracked loan and manage manual repayment status on a separate record (use existing functionality; not a candidate for deletion).
- Provide contextual financial education at points of confusion. Keep secondary actions progressively disclosed rather than adding primary navigation destinations.

## Later / excluded

Usability sessions with real participants and native accessibility verification belong on the evidence backlog, not shown as already complete. Possible later reminders and production operations need separate decisions. Loan offers do not imply lender partnership or approval. Wallet, payments/transfer, verified bank balance, bank synchronization, underwriting, investment, insurance, data imports from real users and production deployment are excluded from this local sprint.

## Demo handoff

Primary route: public sample → local OTP → Explore → A/B/common assumptions → own terms → Save; use Fin as visible contextual assist and demonstrate one explicit finance assumption. Optional tour: synthetic contract review → preview, then a separate already-tracked fixture → record paid. Start clean, show missing fees at least once, and keep private fixture names and values synthetic.

**Gate:** No new primary tab or launch commitment is required; delivery must keep prior affordances discoverable. The contest need for a future accessible demo, if applicable, does not authorize deployment during this sprint.

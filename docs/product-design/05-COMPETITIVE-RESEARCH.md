# 05 · Consumer fintech visual benchmark

**Method:** desk review of publisher screenshots, first-party product
walkthroughs and App Store graphics (26 Nov 2026), not live account
testing. A screenshot establishes what can be seen in that artifact; it
does not verify interactions, accessibility, conversion, or current
versions across platforms. FINORA implications below are design
judgments, not observed user preferences. No competitor imagery,
illustration, logo, or UI assets are imported into FINORA.

## Platform and visualization constraints

- [Apple accessibility HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) and [Apple hit-target guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips/): native targets at least 44 × 44 pt, preserve system text enlargement and comfortable visual separation. [Android accessibility](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps) calls for 48 × 48 dp. Adopt 48 logical units for important custom touch controls where feasible; audit actual hit slop, not just artwork dimensions. Retain dock semantics as well as visual form, and verify keyboard, safe areas, orientation and reduced motion independently of the prototype.
- [Android adaptive layouts](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/layout-and-content/adapt-layout): design for available window width rather than a device label. Stack amount and results in reading order on phones; permit input/results or list/detail panes when space supports them and preserve the same task and focus order.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/REC-WCAG22-20231005/): target AA for web, including 4.5:1 ordinary text, 3:1 large text, 3:1 applicable UI/data marks, visible unobscured keyboard focus, color-independent meaning, and reflow. Publish measured pairs and manually verify real states and surfaces. Color-pair tests and static prototype screenshots are not a conformance audit.
- [Apple chart HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/charts): prioritize informative data, short meaningful axes and accessible descriptions; provide a text/table equivalent and keyboard navigation if interactive. Define units (₫, %/năm, kỳ) explicitly. Amount comparisons use a common zero baseline and identical scenario inputs, or clearly state why different terms cannot be treated as equivalent. Show known principal, interest and fees separately. Unknown is missing, not zero; the graph and its textual alternative must make the same limitation clear.

## Design decisions to validate

1. **Home:** one personalized estimate and one next recorded obligation, labeled for different mental models. Recheck whether users can distinguish both in less than one scroll; add a no-profile state that never invents a number.
2. **AI:** starter prompts are actual jobs; return context and source-aware receipts, not a raw chat wall. Compare two scenarios at the same principal and term and show assumptions before a suggested action.
3. **Simulator:** show the first scheduled payment and total known cost next to their basis, then the schedule details; disclose installment range when installments vary. Avoid a prominent discount or credit-approval signal.
4. **Explore and access:** one strong action per view, stable navigation and a restrained branded moment, not a wallet or bank recreation.
5. **Repayment:** upcoming date → amount → source/status → explicit action; never imply the bank has recognized a manually recorded payment.

**Gate:** first-party visual evidence and concrete application decisions exist across comparable Vietnamese and international apps. This is enough to seed three divergent directions. Owner preference, real-world comprehension, live competitor behavior and native accessibility remain unverified.
